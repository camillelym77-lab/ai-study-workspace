AI Product Management – Sample Notes

Large Language Models (LLMs) generate text by predicting tokens based on context.
Prompt engineering structures instructions, context, constraints and output format.
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) retrieves relevant external information before generation.
Embeddings represent text as vectors so semantically similar content can be compared.
AI agents combine a model with tools, memory or state, and a workflow to complete tasks.
A chatbot mainly responds to messages, while an agent may plan steps and use external tools.
AI product evaluation should consider quality, groundedness, task success, latency, cost and user satisfaction.
Hallucination occurs when a model generates unsupported or incorrect information.
For high-trust use cases, source citations and fallback mechanisms can improve reliability.
Product managers should define where AI creates user value and where rules or human review are more appropriate.
